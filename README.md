### About

Backend developer from Tashkent working in fintech. I build the server side of a network of self-service payment terminals that people use every day to pay for services, top up transit cards and get bank cards. I write mostly in Go and Java.

What I work on:
- Payments end to end: from the terminal to the provider and back, with every payment completed exactly once
- Reliability: idempotent processing, safe retries, no lost or duplicated payments even when networks or devices fail
- Consistency: balances, cash-in and encashment always add up and reconcile
- Terminal fleet: registration, remote management, monitoring and alerts
- Back-office tools: payment search, reports and cancellations

Outside work I explore new technologies and build my own products, pet projects and games. I also solve problems on [LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/u/sultan-djavgashev/).
